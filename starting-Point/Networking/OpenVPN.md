
### What is OpenVPN

- <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">OpenVPN</span> is a type of **VPN** (Virtual Private Network) that allows you to securely connect to another network over the internet. It’s like setting up a <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">secure tunnel</span> between your device and another network or server. This helps to keep your internet activity private and protected from anyone who might try to spy on you (like hackers or your internet service provider).

- So in Short OpenVPN is an open-source software that creates a secure connection over the internet, so you can access the internet or a network safely. It's widely used for **private browsing**, **secure communications**, or even **remote access to a company network**.

### How Does OpenVPN Work?

When you connect to the internet, normally your device (like your phone or laptop) communicates directly with websites and services. But **OpenVPN adds a layer of protection** by encrypting that connection so no one can see what you're doing.

Here’s how the process typically goes:

- **Step 1**: You connect to a VPN server using OpenVPN software. This is usually done by logging into a VPN service (like a provider you subscribe to).
- **Step 2**: The OpenVPN software creates a **secure encrypted tunnel** between your device and the VPN server. This tunnel protects your data from hackers, government surveillance, or anyone else trying to spy on you.
- **Step 3**: Your device sends data (like browsing information, app usage, etc.) through this encrypted tunnel to the VPN server. Since the data is encrypted, no one can see what’s inside it, not even your internet service provider (ISP).
- **Step 4**: The VPN server then communicates with the website or service you’re accessing, and **it looks like the request is coming from the VPN server**, not your device. So, the website only sees the server’s IP address, not yours.
- **Step 5**: When the website responds, the data goes back through the encrypted tunnel to your device.
